  ---Intro:

Love it or hate it, one thing is obvious: the very start of KH1's Gummi Ship segments are monotonous and slow.  It definitely gets better later on, and you'll be able to create some awesome ship designs!

But then...  you decide you want to start KH1 from the beginning again...  and as much fun as you're having reliving Sora's journey, the fact that you're back to the tedious beginnings of KH1 Gummi Ship gameplay, and that you won't be able to use that one ship you made and really liked...  it stings.

But not anymore.

With this tool, you can free that blueprint you made from that old endgame save, and bring it into this new save!  Start your Gummi Journey in style!

(Currently compatible ONLY with foldered PCSX2 saves.  PC Port Save compatibility is being worked on, but is buggy at the moment.)

  ---Tool Functionality:

-Upon opening, there's a "Load Save File" button at the top, with a "Save Changes" button right next to it.  A message box, also at the top, displays the loaded save file, the currently-selected ship, and the current SYS UP and COM LVL.

-Just below the buttons at the top, there are three tabs: "Blueprint Manager", "Gummi Inventory Editor", and "Blueprint Collection".

In "Blueprint Manager", you can manage the ten editable blueprints the game gives you.  This is the default tab.

In "Gummi Inventory Editor", you can freely edit the quantity of any Gummi item in the inventory.

In "Blueprint Collection", you can view and edit your collection of Gummi Ship blueprints obtained from enemy ships and other rewards.

So you can go crazy and max out your inventory if you like, but if all you want is to import a mid-to-lategame blueprint into an early-game save file, and only add to the inventory the neccessary parts to build the ship and make it work, you can do that without ever leaving the Blueprint Manager.

-In the Blueprint Manager, you can Export, Import, Rename, and Delete your Blueprints.  You can even change the save file's currently selected ship!

-You can also add Gummi Blocks to your Inventory based on the requirements of the blueprints currently imported.  Gummi Blocks are catagorized into three types: Common, Rare, and Design.

Common Gummis are Gummi Blocks that can be sold at Cid's shop or are otherwise fairly easy to obtain.

Rare Gummis are Gummi Blocks that, in Final Mix, are obtained from treasure chests and cannot be bought or sold.

Design Gummis are the Gummi Blocks introduced in Final Mix, which means they aren't available in pre-Final Mix versions of the game and may cause unexpected bugs.

By default, Rare and Design Gummis don't get imported, and while it is recommended to leave their checkboxes unchecked so that you aren't locked out of chests containing lategame Gummi Blocks, the user always has the option to import them anyway.
